# M4 — Locking a lab computer: acceptance

The exit gate (MILESTONES §6, M4): CI green on GitHub, the `windows` tests included · **on a real lab computer**:
every way out in the requirements' §1.6 tried and stopped; ExamMode killed from an admin prompt → *stopped
suddenly*, the computer back to normal, ExamMode back on screen 10; the power cord pulled → normal after boot;
`ELECTRON_RUN_AS_NODE` and `--inspect` do nothing with the installed ExamMode.exe; Windows Defender quiet; the
school's firewall rules unchanged.

M4 was built without a lab computer (the teacher, 2026-11-14: *"start M4"*): everything below was proven on
GitHub's Windows Server 2022 (Windows 10's code base) and 2025 (Windows 11's), as administrator. **Nobody has yet
sat at a locked computer and tried to get out.** That is the gate's second half, and it needs the lab.

## 3. What the tests found, and what changed

| # | Finding | What was done |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The exam bar can't be an AppBar while Explorer is stopped.** An AppBar is Explorer's doing; M0's spike tried it with Explorer running. The lock stops Explorer (D7), and then Windows never moved the work area | The bar holds the top of the screen by setting the screen's **work area** itself; Explorer works it out again when it is started. D6's intent is kept: apps fill the space under the bar |
| 2 | **A named pipe made by a service can't be written to by an ordinary account**, and Node can't change that. The lab's account may be an ordinary one (Q12) | ExamMode talks to the Guard on **a port that only this computer can reach** (127.0.0.1:47802) |
| 3 | Who is calling can't be read from such a connection | A caller says which process it is, and the Guard checks that process's program is ExamMode.exe; **only who locked can unlock** (a secret kept in ExamMode's memory). A stranger can't unlock. *To harden in M8* |
| 4 | Blocking outbound traffic does nothing on a profile whose firewall is off | The plan switches the firewall **on** on every profile first, journaled like the rest |
| 5 | The firewall through PowerShell took 3–5 s (M0, finding 4) | Read once through PowerShell, changed through `netsh`: about 2 s for the whole lock |
| 6 | Windows PowerShell 5 failed to load its own modules under the test runner: `PSModulePath` was spelled in another case | Removed whatever its case (M0's finding 0, again) |
| 7 | A thread that waits inside Windows for keys can't be stopped from outside | The keyboard hook's thread is told to quit (`WM_QUIT`) and ends by itself |

## 5. For the teacher

**Things I decided. Each needs your yes or no:**

| # | Case | What I made it do | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **An app closed during the exam** (07) | A strip under the exam bar for 8 seconds, not the card at the top right of the prototype | The bar is the only ExamMode window on the screen then |
| 2 | **Apps without a window** | Left alone, unless they are remote-control tools, AI runners or screen recorders | §2.5; closing every background program would break the computer |
| 3 | **A check that fails** | The exam can't start on that computer; *Check again* after fixing it. The teacher's list says why | F-04 |
| 4 | **ExamMode Guard isn't running** | A failed check: *This computer can't be locked. Tell your teacher.* | An installed ExamMode never runs an exam unlocked |
| 5 | **A development build** | Locks only when told to, and can then be told to allow a virtual machine | So that a teacher's own Mac or a test never locks by accident |
| 6 | **A student who is let back in** | The computer is locked again first, without the checks screen; the apps that aren't part of the exam are closed | F-18: the exam opens by itself |
| 7 | **The receipt screen** | The computer stays locked until *Finish* | F-21 |

**The lab day, for M4** (with M0's run, M2's network check and M3's trial): sit at a locked computer and try every
way out of §1.6; kill ExamMode from an administrator's prompt; pull the power cord; look at Windows Defender's
history; check the school's firewall rules afterwards.
