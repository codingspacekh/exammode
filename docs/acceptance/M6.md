## 3. For the teacher

**Things I decided. Each needs your yes or no:**

| # | Case | What I made it do | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Leave** on the exam bar | A door button at the end of the bar, not a *⋯* menu | The bar is a window 54 px high: a menu can't open inside it. The lock (M4) may change how the bar is made |
| 2 | **After leaving** | The lab computer is back on *Join your exam*. *You can't join this exam again* (10) comes when they try | F-15: the computer goes back to normal |
| 3 | **Add time** (not drawn in the prototype) | *Add time* in the bar offers 5, 10 or 15 minutes for everyone; a student's panel offers the same for them alone | F-41 |
| 4 | **The reason** for Let back in / Keep out | A short list (*The computer crashed*, *The power or the network went*, *Pressed Leave by mistake*, *Closed ExamMode by mistake*) or your own words. One has to be given | F-40: both are written in the log |
| 5 | **After the exam** opens | when you press *End the exam*. When everyone's time is up it doesn't open by itself: a student with extra time may still be working | F-41 |
| 6 | **A student with no contact at the end** | Listed as *No hand-in*, with their latest backup | §2.9 |
| 7 | **The log's spreadsheet** | Time, student, computer, what happened, details. A name that starts with `=` is written so that Excel doesn't take it for a formula | F-42; a student types their own file names |
| 8 | **The sound** | A short beep for a warning, or for someone leaving or asking to come back. On by default | F-39 |
