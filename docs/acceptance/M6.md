# M6 — The teacher's whole exam: acceptance

The exit gate (MILESTONES §6, M6): acceptance and e2e green · every screen matches the prototype at 1280–1920 ·
**N-13**: a new exam from nothing in under 5 minutes, no file edited by hand · the teacher makes and runs a real
exam with one class.

M6 was started before M4 and M5 (the teacher, 2026-11-14: *"continue"*, after being asked whether to build the
lock or these screens first): the lock needs a lab computer to be tried on, and these screens run on the Mac. The
parts of M6 that need the lock or the apps wait for them.

## 3. For the teacher

**Things I decided. Each needs your yes or no:**

| # | Case | What I made it do | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Leave** on the exam bar | A door button at the end of the bar, not a *⋯* menu | The bar is a window 54 px high: a menu can't open inside it. The lock (M4) may change how the bar is made |
| 2 | **After leaving** | The lab computer is back on *Join your exam*. *You can't join this exam again* (10) comes when they try | F-15: the computer goes back to normal |
| 3 | **Add time** (not drawn in the prototype) | *Add time* in the bar offers 5, 10 or 15 minutes for everyone; a student's panel offers the same for them alone | F-41 |
| 4 | **The reason** for Let back in / Keep out | A short list (*The computer crashed*, *The power or the network went*, *Pressed Leave by mistake*, *Closed ExamMode by mistake*) or your own words. One has to be given | F-40: both are written in the log |
| 5 | **After the exam** opens | when you press *End the exam*. When everyone's time is up it doesn't open by itself: a student with extra time may still be working | F-41 |
| 6 | **A student with no contact at the end** | Listed as *No hand-in*, with their latest backup | §2.9 |
| 7 | **The log's spreadsheet** | Time, student, computer, what happened, details. A name that starts with `=` is written so that Excel doesn't take it for a formula | F-42; a student types their own file names |
| 8 | **The sound** | A short beep for a warning, or for someone leaving or asking to come back. On by default | F-39 |
| 9 | **The list of exams** and **the Classes page** (not in the prototype) | Built in the look of the other teacher screens. With nothing kept yet, ExamMode opens on the form for a new exam | N-13; they need your look, as they were never drawn |
| 10 | **Changing a class** after it took an exam | A student still on the list keeps their place in earlier exams (known by their email, else their name). One taken off the list stays on it if an exam has their work | The hand-ins and the log hang on them |
| 11 | **Deleting** | An exam that was run can't be deleted; a class that an exam uses can't either | Their records would go with them |
| 12 | **Spreadsheets** | Pasted rows and CSV files are read. An Excel file (.xlsx) isn't: copy the rows and paste them. I found no class export in the gradebook to match, so the reader takes any columns named like *Name*, *First name*, *Last name*, *Email*, *No* | F-43 |
| 13 | **Files to give** | Any files, by name, without folders. The exam keeps its own copy: changing yours afterwards doesn't change the exam's. A student who is let back in keeps their own copy of a given file | F-33, §2.9 |
| 14 | **The rules** on *Make an exam* | Said as four lines, with no switches | A switch that does nothing would be a lie: every exam follows them in this version |
