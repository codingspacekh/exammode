## 2. For the teacher to read: left, stopped suddenly, or no contact

Every row is a test in `tests/unit/session/attempt.test.ts`. The first eight are the architecture's §2.8 table.
**Please read each row and say if it is what you want.**

| # | What the teacher's laptop sees | What happens | The log says |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Before Start** (checking or ready): Leave, ExamMode stopped, or nothing heard for 10 s | Off the list. They can join again, on any computer | *left before Start* |
| 2 | **Leave**, confirmed, in the exam | **Left** (*pressed Leave*) | *left* |
| 3 | The Guard reports that ExamMode **stopped** | **Left** (*stopped suddenly*), counted from the last time it was heard from | *stopped suddenly* |
| 4 | Nothing heard for **10 s** in the exam | **No contact**. Nothing is decided yet | *no contact* |
| 5 | The **same** ExamMode is heard from again | **In the exam** again, with the gap and what ExamMode recorded during it | *gap, 45 s* |
| 6 | A **new** ExamMode joins for a student who is in the exam or has no contact | **Left** (*stopped suddenly*); the join is refused and waits for you. A new Windows start adds *the computer was restarted* | *stopped suddenly*, *asks to come back* |
| 7 | A student who **left** joins, on any computer | Refused. You are told | *asks to come back* |
| 8 | You press **Let back in** | The waiting join is accepted: **in the exam** on the computer they asked from. Warnings and extra time are kept | *let back in* (your reason), *came back* (time out) |

## 3. Notes for later milestones

- **Process recordings.** `tests/fixtures/procs/` has the two lists recorded on GitHub's Windows runners, and
  `lab-by-hand.json`, written by hand (Office, VS Code running Python, Chrome, AnyDesk) until the lab computers' own
  recordings replace it. That comes with M0's lab run; the recorder now also notes each program's product name,
  which is how a browser renamed `python.exe` is still seen as a browser.
- **The system list** (`src/lib/guard/lists.ts`) is made from the runners' recordings. On them, the only windows
  closed on an idle Windows are Explorer, Windows Terminal and GitHub's own agent. It is checked against a real lab
  computer at M4's gate.
- **The lockdown plan** has one change the architecture's table didn't list: `AutoRestartShell = 0` before Explorer
  is stopped (M0, finding 2).
- **Profiles.** The schema and the matching are done; the real `profiles/*.json` files come in M5. The tests use the
  architecture's VS Code profile and a Word profile.
