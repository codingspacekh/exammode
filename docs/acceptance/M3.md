# M3 — Walking skeleton: acceptance

The exit gate (MILESTONES §6, M3): e2e green · the screens match 01, 03, 04, 07b, 09, 09b, 12, 13 at 1280–1920 ·
**the teacher runs a 10-minute trial with a few students on real lab computers (no locking).**

M3 was started after the teacher tried the clickable prototype (`design/screens/play.html`) and said *"okay go
ahead"* (2026-11-14), with the gates of M0–M2 still open: it runs on the Mac and on GitHub.

**What works now:** `EXAMMODE_MODE=teacher npm run dev` on the teacher's laptop and `npm run dev` on a lab computer
are the first usable ExamMode. A teacher opens an exam; students find it, pick their name, type the code, wait, work, and
hand in; the teacher watches it live and gets every hand-in with its receipt. **Nothing is locked yet** (M4), and
the exam's apps aren't chosen yet (M5).

## 3. For the teacher

**Things I decided. Each needs your yes or no:**

| # | Case | What I made it do | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Teacher mode** before the licence exists (M7) | ExamMode started with `--teacher` (or `EXAMMODE_MODE=teacher`) is the teacher's side; without it, a lab computer's | F-44 comes in M7; until then this is only on your own laptop |
| 2 | **Your name** | Typed on *Make an exam*, and remembered from the last exam | Students see who set the exam; there are no settings yet |
| 3 | **A class**, before classes have their own screen (M6) | Typed on *Make an exam*, one name a line; kept and offered next time | N-13: an exam from nothing in minutes |
| 4 | **Time** | Steps of 5 minutes, up to 10 hours | — |
| 5 | **A website typed without `http://`** | Taken as `https://`. A school page that only answers on `http://` has to be typed with it | Most sites are https |
| 6 | **A student who is refused after leaving** (screen 10 is M6) | The join screen says *You left this exam, so you can't join it again. Tell your teacher.* The teacher can't let them back in from a screen yet (13b is M6) | M3 is the skeleton |
| 7 | **Closing ExamMode's window on a lab computer** during the exam | Nothing stops it yet. The teacher sees *no contact*; starting ExamMode again and joining counts as *stopped suddenly* | The lock is M4 |
| 8 | **A link that opens a new window** | A new tab, if it is one of the exam's pages; otherwise *This page isn't part of the exam* | F-10 |
| 9 | **Downloads, the camera, notifications** in the exam browser | Refused | §2.6 |

**Still to do — the 10-minute trial on real lab computers** (no locking): it needs ExamMode on a few lab computers
and your laptop on the same network, so it is also M2's network check (R3). Until there is an installer (M8) that
means Node and this repository on each of them; it can wait for the lab day with M0's run.

**On your Mac, today:** `npm run build`, then in one terminal `npx electron out/main/index.js --teacher` and in
another `EXAMMODE_USER_DATA=/tmp/exammode-student npx electron out/main/index.js` — a teacher and a student on one
computer.
