05 · In the exam — Word (F-09, F-11, F-14) — pressing Word in the exam bar opens it (or brings it to the front) in the space under the bar; the bar can't be covered. Allowed apps open and save in the student's Exam folder, which holds the files the teacher gave out. A dot shows which apps are open. Alt+Tab only moves between the exam's apps; the Windows key, Task Manager, Win+R and the other shortcuts out do nothing. Word's own ways out — sign-in, online pictures, Copilot, links that open a browser — are turned off for the exam (F-12).
