11b · Add an app to the exam (F-31, F-31a) — Add an app… lists what is installed on the lab computers (each one reports its apps when ExamMode starts), and on how many — an app missing from some computers would leave those students without it. Nothing is hard-coded: next term a teacher can add OneEditor for a video exam or MicroLab for a circuits exam. An app ExamMode knows has an exam profile that also closes its own ways out (sign-in, add-ons, a browser opened from a link); any other app is only allowed to run, which still keeps it off the internet. Choose a program file… adds an app by its .exe.
